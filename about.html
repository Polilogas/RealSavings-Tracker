THE STORY BEHIND THE TRACKER

Make your savings progress visible.

RealSavings Tracker is an independent project created to make saving for personal goals easier to organize and understand. It started with a simple need: a clear place to plan goals, track progress, and keep future purchases in one place.

Why I created RealSavings Tracker

Like many people, I had things I wanted to save for. I wanted a straightforward way to organize those goals, see how much I had saved, and understand how far I still had to go. I also wanted somewhere to keep the things I hoped to buy in the future, without having to manage everything across separate notes and calculations.

I started building RealSavings Tracker to bring those ideas together in one place. Rather than making the process complicated, I wanted to create a tool that helps people give their goals a name, set a target, record their progress, and keep their plans organized.

Seeing progress can give a goal a clearer purpose. A large target may feel far away at first, but tracking each contribution can help you understand the steps between where you are today and where you want to be. Everyone's circumstances are different, and the tool is designed to help you visualize your own journey.

The idea is simple: make your savings goals easier to organize, your progress easier to see, and your plans easier to review. You decide what matters to you and how you want to work toward it.

What you can do with the tracker

RealSavings Tracker is designed for everyday personal savings planning. Whether you are preparing for a trip, a new device, a future purchase, or another personal goal, these features help you keep the details together.

Set personal savings goals

Create goals with names and target amounts so you can keep different plans organized and understand what you are saving toward.

Visualize your progress

Follow the amount saved and see your progress in relation to your target, helping you understand where each goal currently stands.

Record transactions

Keep a record of money added to or removed from a goal so your displayed savings amount reflects the transactions you enter.

Plan with dates

Use start dates and target dates where available to give your goals a timeframe and review your plans as circumstances change.

Keep a wishlist

Organize things you would like to buy in the future so you can keep your ideas together while deciding which goals matter most.

Review reports and statistics

Use the available reports and summaries to review the information you have entered and get a clearer picture of your savings activity.

An independent project with a practical purpose

RealSavings Tracker is built and maintained as an independent project. It is not a financial institution or a bank. The aim is to make a useful everyday tool that is clear, approachable, and practical for people with different savings goals.

I believe basic tools for organizing personal goals should be accessible to everyone. That is why my intention is to keep the tracker's core features free to use, with advertising potentially helping support the ongoing work of maintaining and improving the website.

The project will continue to develop over time. Feedback, real-world use, and practical improvements can help guide which features are worth adding next.

- Michail, Independent Developer

Your information and privacy

RealSavings Tracker is designed to work without requiring you to create an account or register before using the tracker.

The savings information you enter is stored locally in your browser on your device. This means the information is intended to remain in that browser rather than being saved to a personal RealSavings Tracker account. Browser storage can be affected by clearing site data, changing browsers, or using a different device, so you should not assume your information will automatically be available everywhere or recoverable if it is deleted.

The website may use third-party services, such as advertising or analytics tools, if they are enabled. Those services may process information according to their own practices and the website's applicable privacy disclosures.

What may come next
