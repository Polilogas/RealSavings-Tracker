A LITTLE ABOUT US

Saving money should feel more manageable.

RealSavings Tracker is an independent project built around a simple idea: when you have a clear goal and can see your progress, planning your savings can feel less overwhelming.

Why RealSavings Tracker exists

Saving for something important can be difficult when your plans are scattered across notes, calculations, and different accounts. It can also be hard to tell how far you have come or how much you still need.

RealSavings Tracker brings the process into one simple place. You can give a savings goal a name, set a target, record the money you add or remove, and follow your progress over time.

The idea is simple: make your goals easier to understand, your progress easier to follow, and your next step easier to plan.

A straightforward way to plan your savings

Whether you are planning a holiday, saving for a new device, preparing for an expense, or working toward another personal goal, the process starts with a few simple steps.

Built independently, with a practical purpose

RealSavings Tracker is developed independently with an emphasis on clarity and ease of use. The aim is to make a useful everyday tool, keep the experience straightforward, and continue improving it over time.
