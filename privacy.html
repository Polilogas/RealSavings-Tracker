Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how RealSavings Tracker handles information when you visit the website or use the savings-tracking application.

Last updated: October 17, 2026
In short: In the current version of the savings app, your goals and transactions are saved in your browser's local storage rather than in a RealSavings Tracker user account. The website may also load third-party services, including advertising and externally hosted fonts, which operate under their providers' own privacy practices.

1. Who operates RealSavings Tracker?

RealSavings Tracker is an independently developed website and savings-planning tool. For privacy questions about the website, you can contact the developer using the email address provided at the end of this policy.

2. Information stored by the savings app

The current version of the savings app uses the browser's local storage to remember information entered into the app. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Savings goal names and descriptions.
  • Target amounts, current savings, and goal dates.
  • Records of money added to or removed from goals.
  • Application preferences saved by the app.

This information is stored in the browser on the device you are using. The current app does not require a RealSavings Tracker account or use a RealSavings Tracker database to store these goals and transactions.

This does not mean that every request made while visiting the website is private to your device. Website hosting, externally loaded resources, and third-party services may process information as explained below.

3. Local storage and keeping control of your data

Local storage allows the app to remember your information when you return using the same browser and website origin. It is not automatically synchronized with your other devices or browsers.

Your saved data may become unavailable or be deleted if you clear your browser's site data, use a private browsing session, change browsers or devices, or encounter a browser or device problem. We cannot recover data that exists only in your browser if it is lost.

You can use the app's available data-management controls to export a JSON backup, import a compatible backup, or delete saved app data. Keep exported backup files somewhere secure: anyone who can access a file containing your data may be able to read its contents.

Deleting the app's saved data does not necessarily delete cookies, storage, or other information created independently by your browser, hosting provider, or third-party services. You may need to manage those separately through your browser or the relevant provider.

4. Information collected when you visit the website

When your browser loads a website, the hosting provider and the services needed to deliver the page may receive technical information as part of ordinary web requests. Depending on the service, this may include your IP address, browser and device information, requested page, request time, and referring page.

This policy does not claim that the website's hosting provider keeps no logs. The provider's retention and processing practices are governed by its own policies and configuration.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The savings app's own goal and transaction storage uses local storage rather than relying on cookies for those records. However, the website or a third-party service may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies for purposes such as advertising, security, service operation, or remembering choices.

Whether such technologies are used depends on the scripts, settings, and services active on the page. You can review and manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking them may affect some third-party features or services.

Where consent is legally required for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, the relevant consent choices should be provided before those technologies are used. Browser settings alone may not communicate every preference to every provider.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

RealSavings Tracker uses, or may use on pages where advertising is enabled, Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google's advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies and process information such as IP address, browser details, page activity, and advertising identifiers to deliver, measure, limit, or personalize ads, depending on the configuration and your choices.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. To manage Google's personalized advertising settings, visit Google Ads Settings .

Google and its advertising partners may process information under their own privacy policies. Their use of data is not controlled entirely by RealSavings Tracker.

For more information, review Google's information about advertising technologies and Google's Privacy Policy .

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other regions may have additional choices or protections depending on applicable law and the consent tools used on the website.

7. Analytics

The project code reviewed for this policy did not show a separate analytics service such as Google Analytics. This statement should be updated if an analytics tool, tracking tag, or similar service is added or is active elsewhere on the live website.

Advertising measurement, hosting logs, and third-party service activity may still involve technical information even when a separate analytics product is not used.

8. External services and links

The website may load resources from third parties, including Google Fonts and advertising services, and may link to external websites. When your browser requests an external resource or you visit an external link, that provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser information.

External websites and services have their own privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies before using them.

9. How long information is kept

Savings data in local storage generally remains until it is deleted by you, removed by the app, or cleared by your browser or device. Exported backup files remain wherever you choose to store them until you delete them.

Retention of technical information by hosting, advertising, or other third-party providers is determined by those providers' policies and settings.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

Because savings goals and transactions are stored locally in the current app, you can manage those records directly through the app and your browser. For a privacy question or a request concerning information handled by the website operator, please contact us by email. Requests involving an independent third-party provider may need to be directed to that provider.

11. Children's privacy

RealSavings Tracker is a general-purpose savings planning tool and is not designed to request children's personal information. Please contact us if you believe personal information has been provided to the website inappropriately.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, app features, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
