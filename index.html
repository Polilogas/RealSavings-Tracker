YOUR SAVINGS, YOUR DEVICE

A savings tracker designed around simplicity.

You can use RealSavings Tracker to organise your savings without connecting a bank account. Your recorded savings are entered by you, giving you control over the information you choose to track.

1

Enter your own figures

Record the amounts you want to track. The tracker is intended for organising savings goals, not automatically importing bank transactions.

2

Keep data stored locally

Your savings information is stored in your browser on your device rather than automatically synchronised to an online account.

3

Understand the trade-off

Local storage means your information may not appear on another device or browser. Clearing browser data can also remove locally stored information, so take care when managing it.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

About RealSavings Tracker.

Here are answers to common questions about using a free savings goal tracker and organising your plans.

Is RealSavings Tracker free?

Yes. RealSavings Tracker is free to use for organising your savings goals and recording your progress.

What can I track with the app?

You can create goals for holidays, technology, home purchases, emergency savings, and other things you want to save for. You decide which goals to create and what amounts to record.

Can I track more than one savings goal?

Yes. You can organise separate goals for different priorities, making it easier to review each target individually.

Does the tracker connect to my bank?

The tracker is designed for manually recording your savings. You enter deposits and withdrawals yourself rather than relying on automatic bank transaction imports.

Where is my savings information stored?

Your savings information is stored locally in your browser. It is not automatically synced between devices. Clearing your browser's stored data may remove your records, so be careful when clearing site data or changing devices.

Will the tracker guarantee that I reach my goal?

No. The tracker helps you organise your plan and follow the amounts you record, but your progress depends on your circumstances and the money you are able to save.

How do I get started?

Open the tracker, create a goal, enter your target amount, and start recording your savings. You can begin with one goal and add more when you're ready.
