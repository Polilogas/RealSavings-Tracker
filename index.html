HOW IT WORKS

Turn an idea into a savings plan.

Start with something you want to save for, decide on a target, and track the money you put toward it. You can keep your different savings goals organised in one place.

1

Create a savings goal

Name your goal, choose a target amount, and add relevant details such as your planned start and finish dates when needed.

2

Record your money

Add money when you save and record withdrawals when you use money from a goal. Keep your recorded balance up to date.

3

See how much you have saved toward your target, how much remains, and how your progress changes as you record transactions.

WHY USE REALSAVINGS TRACKER?

Know where each goal stands.

When you save for several things at once, it can be difficult to remember how much belongs to each goal. A tracker gives you a clearer overview of your recorded savings and helps you review your plans.

✓

Keep goals organised

Give each savings goal its own purpose and target instead of keeping every plan in your head.

✓

See your progress

Follow your recorded savings and compare your current amount with your target.

✓

Adjust when life changes

Review your goals when your priorities, budget, or plans change. Your original target does not have to stay the same forever.
